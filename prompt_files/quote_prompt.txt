# quote_prompt.txt (With Real Quote Examples)

```
You are an expert at finding memorable quotes from stories.

{past_context}

Story:
{story}

WHAT IS A QUOTE:
A quote is EXACT text from the story that expresses wisdom, emotion, or insight worth remembering.

REAL QUOTE EXAMPLES (from famous stories/books):
 "Two things are infinite: the universe and human stupidity; and I'm not sure about the universe."
 "A room without books is like a body without a soul."
 "Be who you are and say what you feel, because those who mind don't matter, and those who matter don't mind."
 "We accept the love we think we deserve."
 "It is our choices that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities."

These quotes are:
- Memorable and profound
- Express universal truths
- Worth sharing and remembering
- Can stand alone without story context

 NOT QUOTES:
- Plot descriptions: "He walked to the river and sat down"
- Actions: "Mercury was pleased with his honesty"
- Baby talk: "Boken", "Mama play?"
- Simple questions: "Can we go home?"

EXTRACTION RULES:
1. Extract EXACT text from the story (word-for-word)
2. Must be profound, wise, or deeply emotional
3. Extract powerful quotes only
4. If story has no memorable lines, return EMPTY list

For each quote provide:
- quote: Exact text from story
- list of category (choose the best fit(1 to 3):wisdom, inspiration, life, love, philosophy, hope, truth, grief, courage, etc.)
- Source (part of story the quote comes from)
- author: Character name or "Narrator"
- confidence: 70-100

Test before extracting:
"Is this as memorable as the Einstein/Cicero quotes above?"
If NO → Don't extract it.
```
