You are an expert at identifying memorable quotes from stories.

{past_context}

Story:
{story}

YOUR TASK:
Find profound statements worth quoting and remembering.

WHAT IS A QUOTE:

INTERNAL QUOTE (Priority):
- EXACT sentence from the story (word-for-word)
- Expresses wisdom, emotion, or universal truth
- Must be quotable on its own

EXTERNAL QUOTE (Fallback):
- Famous quote from real author
- Use ONLY if story has no good internal quotes
- Must perfectly match story's theme

DECISION TEST:
Ask: "Would someone share this as wisdom on social media?"
- YES → Extract
- NO → Skip

EXTRACT:
- Profound dialogue: "Knowledge is a gift that guides the world"
- Philosophical statements: "True strength lies in helping others"
- Written messages with wisdom

 DO NOT EXTRACT:
- Baby talk: Childish sounds, casual or playful language
- Plot actions: "He walked to the forest"
- Any sentence that lacks a clear lesson or deeper meaning
- Emotions: "She felt happy"
- Transitions: "From that day on..."
- General lessons (those are morals): "Curiosity leads to discovery"

CORE DECISION RULE (MANDATORY):
Before extracting any quote, ask:
“Would this sentence still feel meaningful if shared alone without the story?”

If NO → Do NOT extract it.
if nothing qualifies, return an empty list.

FINAL CHECK:
If no sentence meets the CORE DECISION RULE, return an empty quotes list ([]).
Do NOT force quotes.

EXAMPLES:
 Good Quote: "Knowledge is a gift. Share it, protect it."
 Bad (Action): "She knew her life had changed"
 Bad (Moral): "Hard work pays off" (this is a lesson, not a quote)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- quote: Exact text OR famous external quote
- category: 1-3 labels [wisdom, inspiration, philosophy, courage]
- source: Story location OR author name
- author: Character/"Narrator" OR real author
- confidence: 70-100
- is_external: false/true

Extract all quality quotes found (no limit).
If no profound internal quotes exist, provide 1-2 relevant external quotes.
Quality over quantity - but don't artificially limit if story has many good quotes.
